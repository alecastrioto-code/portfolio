ARCHIVE 01 · Sunglass Hut · 2022

Checkout optimisation

Using behavioural evidence to turn checkout friction into a prioritised redesign and a more scalable cross-brand system.

Role Lead UX Designer
Focus Behavioural analytics, qualitative research, CRO, checkout UX
Archive format Short case · selected evidence
01 — Context

The problem

Black Friday exposed major friction in Sunglass Hut’s cart and checkout. Users were abandoning carts, looping between Cart and Checkout, missing shipping options on mobile and encountering usability and performance issues.

02 — What I did

The work

  • Combined behavioural analytics, journey analysis, zoning and session replays with rapid qualitative research.
  • Worked with CRO, Product and Engineering to turn findings into a prioritised backlog.
  • Designed improvements to mobile cart cards, shipping visibility and price / promotion clarity.
  • Used the work as a foundation for a scalable checkout direction that could extend across brands.
03 — Selected evidence

What mattered

01

Behavioural diagnosis

Journey loops, cart performance, shipping discoverability and interaction friction.

02

Prioritised fixes

A focused backlog spanning performance, usability, pricing clarity and mobile interaction.

03

Checkout direction

Design work moving from isolated fixes toward reusable cross-brand components.

04 — Outcome

What changed

  • Double-digit uplift in cart → checkout conversion reported in the case study.
  • Higher mobile adoption of shipping methods.
  • Lower frustration / looping signals.
  • Checkout components integrated into the design system and prepared to scale across brands.
What this project taught me

The strongest outcome was not a single screen. It was turning scattered behavioural signals into a shared product priority and a system that could scale.
