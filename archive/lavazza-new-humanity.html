ARCHIVE 04 · Armando Testa × Lavazza · 2020

The New Humanity

Turning a physical calendar and hundreds of artistic contributions into a digital exploration experience during lockdown.

Role UX Designer
Focus Information architecture, card sorting, exploration patterns, interface design
Archive format Short case · selected evidence
01 — Context

The problem

Lavazza’s New Humanity project gathered artistic contributions about the human experience during the pandemic. The challenge was to translate a paper calendar and a large, diverse body of work into a digital experience that still felt emotional and exploratory.

02 — What I did

The work

  • Ran a card-sorting workshop to create semantic categories and tags for the artistic contributions.
  • Defined two exploration modes: sequential calendar navigation and freer thematic discovery.
  • Categorised content types into reusable atoms / modules to support a scalable content system.
  • Designed the website interface as an interactive, sensorial extension of the campaign.
03 — Selected evidence

What mattered

01

Information architecture

A semantic structure for a large and varied set of artistic contributions.

02

Exploration patterns

Sequential calendar navigation paired with thematic free discovery.

03

Modular content

Reusable content atoms and modules designed to simplify ongoing development.

04 — Outcome

What changed

  • The source portfolio describes roughly 200 artistic contributions from an international group of artists.
  • The site continued to grow with new contributions.
  • The project helped establish the annual calendar as an ongoing digital experience.
What this project taught me

A visually expressive experience still needed rigorous architecture underneath it. Structure was what made the creative concept maintainable.
