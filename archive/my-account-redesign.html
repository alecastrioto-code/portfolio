ARCHIVE 03 · Sunglass Hut · 2021

My Account redesign

Redesigning login, registration, password recovery and account patterns to reduce friction and create reusable form behaviour.

Role Senior UX Designer
Focus User flows, forms, errors, accessibility, account UX
Archive format Short case · selected evidence
01 — Context

The problem

The work focused on registration, login, password recovery and the account dashboard. High error rates and inconsistent flows made core account tasks harder than they needed to be.

02 — What I did

The work

  • Synthesised qualitative findings and collected requirements across account flows.
  • Simplified flows, reduced unnecessary steps and improved post-login redirection.
  • Worked with Engineering to catalogue and redesign error scenarios, validation and field feedback.
  • Defined new form-design guidelines covering dynamic labels, helpers, trailing icons, keyboard behaviour and accessibility.
03 — Selected evidence

What mattered

01

Flow optimisation

Reduced steps and preserved user context before / after authentication.

02

Designing for errors

Clearer error language, updated validation timing and more consistent field states.

03

Reusable form system

Interaction and UI patterns intended for use beyond the account area.

04 — Outcome

What changed

  • +200% logged sessions reported after deployment.
  • +37% AOV for logged users.
  • +18% orders from logged sessions.
  • 0.1% form error rate and 90% first-try password recovery reported in the original portfolio.
What this project taught me

The redesign became more valuable when it stopped being only an account project and started defining form patterns that could be reused across the wider site.
