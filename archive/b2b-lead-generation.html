ARCHIVE 06 · Brandsdistribution · 2019

B2B lead generation

Moving beyond endless A/B testing to understand why prospective B2B customers were dropping out of the acquisition funnel.

Role UX/UI Designer
Focus User research, funnel analysis, Hotjar, landing pages, experimentation
Archive format Short case · selected evidence
01 — Context

The problem

The acquisition funnel was underperforming despite continuous A/B testing. An initial audit showed that the problem was broader than page variants: technical issues, usability issues and content / proposition clarity were all contributing to drop-off.

02 — What I did

The work

  • Combined interviews, moderated tests, heatmaps, web analytics and Customer Service logs.
  • Mapped the acquisition journey and highlighted funnel drop-offs and pain points.
  • Used Hotjar attention patterns to improve skimmability, microcopy and imagery.
  • Created a strategic document that linked funnel phases, priorities and KPIs, then informed new landing-page experiments.
03 — Selected evidence

What mattered

01

Research mix

Interviews, moderated testing, heatmaps, analytics and support evidence.

02

Journey strategy

A shared funnel view connecting pain points to actions and KPIs.

03

Landing-page iteration

Content and layout changes based on observed attention and user concerns.

04 — Outcome

What changed

  • -10% bounce rate reported in the original portfolio.
  • -7% customer acquisition cost.
  • +18% conversion rate.
What this project taught me

Experimentation became more useful once it was driven by an understanding of users rather than by a constant supply of page variants.
